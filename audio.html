The Stadium sounds different when it’s empty. No crowd, no vendors, no announcements..just the hum of the lights and the occasional sound of something moving somewhere down the hall. Room 161 is usually quiet too, except for an old radio that’s been sitting on the same shelf for years. Nobody remembers where it came from, but it still works.
